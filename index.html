Prepare Your File for a Clear Print

A clear source file gives the printer more usable information. Use the original downloaded PDF or high-resolution image where available.

If you photograph a physical document, place it flat under even lighting and hold the camera directly above it. Keep all edges visible and inspect the saved image at full size.

Before uploading, confirm that:

  • Names and numbers are readable.
  • Photographs are clear.
  • QR codes retain their complete border.
  • Front and back files are correctly identified.
  • No unrelated private information is included.

Printing cannot reliably restore information that is blurred, cropped or missing from the source.
